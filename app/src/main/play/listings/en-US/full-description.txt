Play and analyze Go games with KataGo.
Play people around the world with your OGS (online-go.com) account.
Following the OGS terms of service, the AI is turned off during OGS games.
Completely free, ad-free, and open source. No registration needed for analysis and games against KataGo.
This is not an official OGS app.
